Bingo of Bias

Spot the stereotypes hiding in everyday ads.

How to play

1

An ad plays on your screen automatically, at the same moment for everyone.

2

Above the video you get a hint, not the answer. Work out which bias it points to.

3

Tap the tile you think is right. Correct tiles stay marked for good. A wrong tap turns red until the next ad.

4

Your 4x4 card is uniquely yours. Win by completing any one full line: row, column or diagonal.
